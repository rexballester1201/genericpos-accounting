# Finding your way

## Signing in
**Who:** everybody with an account.

1. Open the address of the system.
2. Type your **email or username** and your **password**.
3. Select **Sign in**.

Forgotten the password? Select **Forgot your password?** and follow the link that arrives by email. Too many wrong tries lock the account for a while; wait, or ask an administrator to reset it.

> **Note:** A session lasts as long as you keep using it and is refreshed quietly in the background. If you sign out on one device, that device alone is signed out.

## The parts of the screen

- **The sidebar on the left** holds the work, in groups: Ledger, Sales, Purchases, Cash and assets, Planning, Reports, Administration, Help. You see only what your role may open.
- **The bar along the top** shows where you are, the bell for notifications, and your own menu — your account, the light or dark theme, and signing out.
- **A small number beside a sidebar item** counts things waiting for you: entries to approve, your entries that were returned.
- **On a phone**, the sidebar hides behind the ☰ button at the top left.

## The dashboard
**Who:** everybody. **Where:** the house at the top of the sidebar, or the company name.

It answers "where do we stand today?" in one screen:

- **Cash and banks, Receivables, Payables, Net income year to date** — each is a link to the report behind it.
- **What needs a person** — entries waiting for approval, entries returned to you, months that have ended but are still open, bank statements not reconciled, depreciation not yet run.
- **Revenue and expenses, month by month** for the fiscal year.
- **Your work** — your drafts, what you submitted, what came back, and drafts of invoices, bills and receipts.
- **How the books read today** — the current ratio, the quick ratio, debt-to-equity and the net margin, each beside the same date a year earlier, and the year against the budget.
- **Latest postings** — the last entries that reached the ledger.

## Finding things

- Every list has a search box and filters across the top: status, dates, book, customer, and so on. The filters go into the address, so you can bookmark a view or send it to a colleague.
- Numbers, names and codes are all searchable: type *INV-000041*, *Pili*, or *1121*.
- A list you can act on has its buttons at the top right of each row or on the page header.

## Printing
**Who:** everybody.

Every report has a **Print** button. The printed sheet drops the sidebar and the buttons and prints as an A4 page with:

- the company's name, registered name, address and TIN at the top (the letterhead),
- the title of the report and the period it covers,
- **Prepared by** — you — and the **Checked by**, **Approved by** and **Noted by** lines from **Settings › Printed reports**,
- "Printed by … on …" at the foot, and the page number.

Wide reports — the monthly income statement, the worksheet, the columnar books, income by department — print landscape by themselves.

> **Tip:** In the browser's print dialog, choose "Save as PDF" to file a report or email it.

## Spreadsheets
Every report has **Download CSV**. The file opens straight in Excel or LibreOffice: it carries the company name, the title, the period and the currency at the top, then the figures as plain numbers with no currency signs, so you can total them.

## Attachments
Journal entries, invoices, bills, receipts, payments and assets each have an **Attachments** panel: the scanned official receipt, the supplier's invoice, the contract. Select **Attach a file** (PDF, a picture, or a spreadsheet, up to 10 MB). Only people signed in can open them — the files are never served by a plain web address.

## Your account
**Where:** your name at the top right › **My account**.

Change your name, your username and your email, upload a picture, and change your password. Changing your email asks for your current password first, and the old address is told — that is deliberate: it stops somebody with a stolen session from taking the account over.

## When the connection drops
The screens you have already opened keep working, and the last figures you loaded stay on screen. Anything that writes — posting, saving, approving — waits for the connection and tells you plainly that it could not reach the server. Nothing is half-written: a change either reaches the books or does not.

## Common problems

**"Session expired. Please sign in again."** You were away long enough for the session to end, or an administrator changed your account. Sign in again.

**"Too many incorrect password attempts."** The account is locked briefly. Wait the minutes it names, or ask an administrator.

**A screen says you do not have access.** Your role does not cover it. The table in [Welcome](01-welcome.md) says which role does.
